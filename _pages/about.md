## About Me

Hello! I'm <strong>Roman Yoder</strong>, a mechanical engineer and software developer passionate about robotics, automation, and creative problem solving. I thrive on interdisciplinary projects that bridge the gap between hardware and software, and I love bringing new technology from concept to reality.

My experience spans a variety of fields and challenges, from medical robotics to industrial automation and research-driven innovation.
